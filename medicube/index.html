Concept film, work in progress

Booster Pro X2: one scene per mode

Each of the device's modes gets its own short 3D scene: a material that behaves the way the mode's official one-line copy reads. The scenes are built and rendered from Python scripts in Blender, written with Claude Code. Still to come: the DUAL scene, the full film cut, and a web page.

Unofficial concept work. Not affiliated with or endorsed by medicube or APR. Mode names and copy lines come from the official product page with the "2X" claims left out. The images are metaphors and make no efficacy claims.

Process: the production log (in Korean) and the Blender and compositing scripts.
